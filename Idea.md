# UrbanInfraAI: Smart Urban Infrastructure Planning Agent

**UrbanInfraAI** is an AI-powered decision-support platform designed to help city planners, architects, and policymakers make more informed, data-driven decisions about urban infrastructure. It integrates diverse data sources—like satellite imagery, census data, GIS layers, and traffic feeds—to forecast future needs and recommend optimal infrastructure placements.

## Key Capabilities

- Predict urban growth (population, traffic, housing, pollution).
- Recommend infrastructure sites (roads, schools, hospitals, utilities).
- Evaluate sustainability (carbon footprint, green spaces, resilience).
- Ensure inclusive access to services (identify underserved zones).

## Why It Matters

Urban planning today is slow, inefficient, and often inequitable. UrbanInfraAI bridges the gap between static plans and dynamic urban realities—helping cities grow smarter, greener, and fairer.

## Example Use Case

A municipality wants to add 3 new government schools. UrbanInfraAI analyzes population clusters, road access, underserved zones, and travel times to suggest optimal locations with supporting maps and equity scores.
